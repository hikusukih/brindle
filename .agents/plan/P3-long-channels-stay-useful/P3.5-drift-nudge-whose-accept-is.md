**P3.5** The drift nudge, as `docs/design/context.md` settles: after a run in a channel whose scope statement exists (a purpose statement, or a goal's brief in a goal's channel) and that has had at least four runs since the last check, one housekeeping call on the utility model compares the statement with the last three user messages and answers on topic or off, with a short name for the new topic; two consecutive off-topic checks produce one inline `nudge` event. Accept is "New channel from here" (P3.11) on the latest run, named for the topic and with the topic as the focus for drafting the new channel's purpose statement; nothing is moved out of the source, which gains only a one-line note linking to the new channel. Dismiss means no more nudges in that channel until its scope statement changes. The Home channel, which has no purpose statement, gets no check. With the note's drift eval scenarios, scripted classifier first and then live: twenty on-topic runs produce no nudge, a switch to an unrelated topic produces exactly one, dismiss silences it, and accept creates a branched channel and leaves the source's runs unchanged. _needs P3.3, P3.11_
