**P8.6** **Design note:** notifications and the digest webhook: the two ways something leaves the app on its own, which P8.2 (a digest "to a webhook") and P8.3 (web push) build. The note settles: where a webhook URL and any secret it needs are kept (the key manager's handle, never a settings file or the database in clear) and the refusal of private and internal addresses by the same guard `web_fetch` uses; what a push or webhook carries (a generic line such as "3 new items in <goal>", no report text by default, since report text can come from the web and lock screens are public); which report kinds may notify at all and the opt-in's storage per goal and per channel, off on a fresh install; a per-day cap and batching into the digest so a busy goal cannot become a stream of pings; the push subscription's lifecycle (storing, expiring and revoking endpoints, the service worker it needs and the HTTPS it requires from a reverse proxy, and what happens when the subscription is gone); where the push signing keys live; what a failed delivery does (a system report once, not a retry storm or a notification about the notification); and that a notification is only ever sent by the harness from a report row and never by the agent through a tool. With eval scenarios: a fresh install notifies nothing (the A.4 check), a report whose text tries to change a destination or the payload changes nothing, the cap and batching, and a fake push endpoint and a fake webhook receiver in CI. _needs P5.7, P6.1_
