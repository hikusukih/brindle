**P6.11** The vault screen, reached from Settings: every stored secret by name and kind with its scope (hosts, tools, goals or channels, expiry, whether each use needs approval) and when it was last used, never its value or any part of it; revoke on each, which takes effect on the next use; and the `secret_audit` log, newest first, filterable by secret and by outcome, with refusals marked, as `docs/design/key-manager.md` says it is shown. Narrowing a scope here is allowed and widening it is not, since widening goes through a request card. With tests that no response from the screen's endpoints holds a value, that a revoked secret is refused on the next use and the refusal is audited, and that the endpoints need the user's session like the vault form. _needs P6.3, P6.5, P2.14_
