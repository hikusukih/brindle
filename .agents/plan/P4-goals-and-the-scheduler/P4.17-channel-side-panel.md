**P4.17** The channel side panel: a panel in every channel's view, collapsed by default and opened from the channel header (a drawer on a phone), so it takes no room from the conversation. In every channel it lists the artifacts the channel's runs made. It is also where the goal section of P4.17a and the actions of P4.17b appear in a goal's channel, and where reports (P5.9) and goal links (P4.14) join when they land. _needs P4.3, P4.18, P2.4a_
