**P4.12** Runs gain a goal scope and a schedule trigger; the server builds the scheduler with hooks that create a goal-scoped run from a fire's task prompt and report a goal as busy while it has a run going, and starts the poll and `recover()` at startup, reading cron in the user's time zone from P4.22. Scheduled runs get the 12-hour approval timeout, ending as "approval not given". It also hands the scheduler to the places that were built to take it and have nothing to give it until now: it registers `propose_schedule` (P4.5), passes the time-zone setting's change to `Scheduler.setTimeZone` (P4.22), and gives the goal archive, restore and delete routes the scheduler (P4.11).
