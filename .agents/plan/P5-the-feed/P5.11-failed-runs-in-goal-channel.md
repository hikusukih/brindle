**P5.11** Failed runs where the user looks: a scheduled run that failed, was missed or stopped on a limit shows as a system line in the goal's channel, taken from its P5.8 report, and as a marker on the goal's tile on the Goals screen that opens the channel at that line. The line says what happened and when, in plain words; it is written by the harness from a template, with no model call. It needs no Home channel and no side panel, so it can land before P5.9. _needs P5.8, P4.3a, P4.18_
